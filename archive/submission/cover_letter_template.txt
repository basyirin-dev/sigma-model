We are submitting the manuscript entitled "The Σ-Model: Schema Coherence, Cognitive
Faculty Evaluation, and Phase-Structured Curriculum Design for AI Agents" for
consideration in the Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research.

This paper introduces a formal dynamical-systems framework for understanding and
ameliorating compositional generalisation failure in neural agents. The core
contribution is the Schema Coherence (Σ-Model) framework, which:
  (1) Formalises schema coherence σ_A as a latent state variable governed by an
      autocatalytic ODE with a transcritical bifurcation (R_0 = 1);
  (2) Derives nine falsifiable predictions experimentally validated on a synthetic
      compositional benchmark;
  (3) Proposes a phase-structured curriculum that closes the OOD generalisation gap
      from 45.6pp to 0.0pp (Cohen's d = 9.1).

The supplementary code archive contains the full implementation, a minimal reproducible
example (scripts/mre_sigma.py, 0.5s CPU), and all experimental configurations. The
manuscript, code, and data are fully reproducible via the provided Dockerfile.

We confirm that this work is original, has not been published previously, and is not
under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree
with its submission to JAIR.
