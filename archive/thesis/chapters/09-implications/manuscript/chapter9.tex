\chapter{What Schema Coherence Can and Cannot Tell Us About Alignment}
\label{ch:implications}

\textit{This chapter is under development (Paper~09, final scoping review). Reframed
per external assessment: it opens with the question ``What does the evidence justify
concluding about alignment?'', states limitations before implications, and argues for
schema-coherent training as a \emph{candidate component} of a broader alignment
strategy — not ``the optimal path to safe AGI''. Corrigibility, CEV, and indirect
normativity are treated as long-term programme implications.}
