# 1. Pivot Overview

## 1.1 Original Context

The Σ-Model project began as a dynamical-systems framework for studying **schema coherence ($\sigma_A$)** in neural network training, originally applied to **protein domain architecture recombination**. The project produced:

- A formal ODE system governing training dynamics ($\delta_A, \beta_A, \sigma_A, \alpha_A, \hat{M}_A, \Xi_A$)
- Transcritical bifurcation analysis at $R_0 = 1$ (the "basic schema reproduction number")
- A five-phase training arc characterizing the $\sigma$-trap
- Pilot experiments confirming the $\sigma$-trap's existence and the efficacy of $\sigma_A$ coupling
- A paper submitted to the **Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research (JAIR)** on 24 June 2026

The core scientific contribution — the ODE framework, the $\sigma$-trap discovery, and the bifurcation analysis — remains valid and is not an error of research. What changed was the **target domain** for applying this formalism.

## 1.4 The Sigma-Model's Role

The Sigma-Model paper is **not invalidated** by this pivot. Its formal ODE framework, $\sigma$-trap discovery, and bifurcation analysis map directly onto AGI safety problems:

| Sigma-Model Concept | Maps To |
|:-------------------|:---------|
| $\sigma$-trap (low $\sigma_A$ equilibrium) | Mesa-optimization and deceptive alignment |
| Transcritical bifurcation at $R_0 = 1$ | Threshold theorem for alignment phase transition |
| Five-phase training arc | Capability generalization vs. safety generalization dynamics |
| Cognitive faculty benchmarks | AGI evaluation and safety case frameworks |

The pivot is from **application domain** (proteins → safety), not from **methodology**.

## 1.5 The Name

**Σ-Align** (Sigma-Align) — preserves the $\sigma$ (schema coherence) formalism from the Σ-Model while signaling the new direction: alignment. The name allows future extensions (Σ-Interpret, Σ-Certify, Σ-Proof) without namespace exhaustion.
