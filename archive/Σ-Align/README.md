# Σ-Align

**Schema-Coherent Alignment** — extending the Σ-Model formalism to AGI safety and alignment.

## Origin

This folder documents the research domain selection process that led to the pivot from the original Σ-Model (protein domain architecture recombination) toward **Artificial General Intelligence Safety and Alignment** — specifically the **Long-Term Agency, Goal Preservation, and Existential Risk Reduction** specialization, culminating in **Coherent Extrapolated Volition (CEV) and Indirect Normativity**.

The decision was formalized through two successive Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) frameworks:
1. A 22-criteria weighted sum model (initial evaluation)
2. A 21-criteria 2-additive Choquet integral with MCMC uncertainty propagation (final evaluation)
