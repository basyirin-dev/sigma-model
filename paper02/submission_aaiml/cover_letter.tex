\textbf{Research Context and Main Contributions:} \\
A fundamental unresolved problem in machine learning is the tendency of deep neural networks to exploit surface statistical shortcuts rather than learning systematic compositional rules. In this paper, we formulate a continuous dynamical-systems framework---the \emph{Two-Subspace Phase-Boundary Framework}---to model representation formation under competitive loss objectives. 

Our core findings and contributions include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Analytical Transcritical Bifurcation:} We prove that the transition from shortcut entrapment ($E_S$) to schema-coherent representation formation ($E_C$) is governed by an analytical transcritical bifurcation at the exact structural pressure threshold $\lambda_{\text{crit}} = b_C / a_C$, formalizing the non-dimensional threshold ratio $R_0 = \lambda a_C / b_C = 1$.
    \item \textbf{Large-Scale Multi-Benchmark Empirical Validation:} Across 960 controlled production runs ($n=30$ seeds per cell for Tier 1 primary runs and $n=10$ seeds for Tier 2 architecture scaling) spanning four compositional benchmark suites ($\hbar$ Homomorphic Algebra, SCAN \texttt{jump}, COGS structural parsing, and PCFG-SET) and three architecture families (Transformer 2L, Transformer 4L, and recurrent GRU Seq2Seq), we confirm the predicted sharp change-point separatrix ($k \in [58.2, 72.4] \gg 15.0$, $R^2 > 0.91$). Primary Intent-to-Treat (ITT) population reporting ($72.1\% \pm 10.1\%$ at $\lambda=0.025$) and conditional escaped sub-cohort equivalence ($\Delta \mu = 0.35\%$, TOST $p < 0.001$) decisively falsify smooth regularizer alternatives ($k < 5.0$).
    \item \textbf{Exact Binomial Reversibility and Anti-Grokking Stability:} We demonstrate $100\%$ late-onset recovery of trapped models upon activating supercritical pressure at step $t=1000$ (exact Clopper-Pearson 95\% CI $[88.4\%, 100.0\%]$), and prove through extended-horizon $20{,}000$-step evaluations that subcritical models remain stably arrested without spontaneous grokking within the evaluated finite horizon.
    \item \textbf{Econometric VAR Precedence and Curvature Bounds:} Using Centered Kernel Alignment (CKA) and whitened Gradient Cosine Alignment (GCA), we prove via Augmented Dickey-Fuller stationarity and vector autoregressive (VAR) econometric testing that representation manifold alignment predictively precedes behavioral out-of-distribution accuracy jumps by $\approx 150$ steps ($F = 3.716, p < 0.01, \Delta R^2 = 0.184$), with Hessian eigenvalues strictly bounded below the Edge of Stability ceiling ($\lambda_{\text{max}} \le 1680.4 \ll 2/\eta = 2000.0$).
\end{enumerate}
