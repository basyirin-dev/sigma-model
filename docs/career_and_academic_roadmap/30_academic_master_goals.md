## The 30 Master Goals Registry

### Pillar I: Research Output, Publications & Scientific Impact
1. **Publication Cadence:** Publish 2–4 first-author papers per year in top venues (*NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, JMLR, PRL, Physical Review E, Nature Machine Intelligence*) post-PhD. As an undergraduate, secure a first workshop or conference paper by year 2 or 3.
2. **Field-Shaping Papers:** Publish one genuinely field-shaping paper every 2–3 years that redefines learning dynamics, compositional generalization, or AI-for-science.
3. **Citation & h-index Trajectory:** Reach an $h$-index of 25–35 by age 35, and 50–60 by age 50, driven by 4–6 foundational, highly cited masterworks.
4. **Named Theorem / Framework:** Develop a named theorem, framework, algorithm, or model (e.g., *The $\Sigma$-Bifurcation Law*, *Basri Threshold*) adopted by 100+ research groups and cited 1,000+ times.

### Pillar II: Academic Degrees, Mathematical Maturity & Education
6. **Rigorous PhD:** Earn a rigorous PhD in computer science, mathematics, or a joint program, with PhD-level working knowledge across three core domains: ML theory, dynamical systems/quantum mechanics, and advanced mathematics.
7. **Advanced Mathematics Core:** Master graduate coursework in measure theory, functional analysis, differential geometry, probability, statistical mechanics, quantum information, and optimization.
9. **Self-Directed Mathematical Maturity:** Cultivate deep mathematical maturity in quantum mechanics, dynamical systems, and learning theory through research and rigorous self-study.

### Pillar III: Community, Workshops, Open Science & Global Network
5. **International Collaboration:** Build a collaboration network spanning 30–50 countries, with active co-authorship in 10–20 countries per year.
10. **Technical Blog & Lecture Notes:** Create an open-access technical blog and lecture-note series on "Physics of Intelligence" and "Learning Dynamics"; later launch an open-access journal/niche series.
11. **Flagship Recurring Workshop:** Found and chair a recurring workshop at NeurIPS/ICML/ICLR on compositionality, neural dynamics, and AI for physical sciences.
12. **Distributed Global Lab:** Build a distributed research lab spanning five continents using cloud/remote open infrastructure.
15. **Open Science & Translations:** Release all papers, codebases, and lecture notes openly, with key works community-translated into 5–10 major languages.
17. **Monthly Technical Essays:** Publish one substantial technical essay, lecture note, or deep-dive per month; compile yearly into an open volume.
18. **Invited Talks & Deep Work:** Deliver 10–20 invited talks per year at top institutions, while strictly bounding conference travel (2–4/year) to protect deep-work focus.
19. **High-Impact Public Lectures:** Deliver seminal public lectures/TEDx talks, prioritizing seminal papers and tools over promotional speaking.
20. **Keynote Addresses:** Deliver 5–10 keynotes per year at major conferences, symposia, or summer schools at mid-career.
21. **Annual AI-for-Science Symposium:** Organize an annual international symposium on neural dynamics and scientific AI.
22. **Deep Institutional Ties:** Maintain active co-authorships across 25–40 countries, with deep collaborative ties to 5–10 leading world labs.
23. **Global Open-Source Community:** Build an open-source contributor community across 100+ countries around software libraries (`sigma_align`), benchmarks, and tutorials.

### Pillar IV: Books, Monographs & Public Impact
13. **Monograph Series Editor:** Partner with a top university press to edit a monograph series in Mathematical Machine Learning and AI-for-Science.
14. **Graduate Textbook & Monograph:** Author a definitive graduate-level textbook/monograph by late 30s, plus 2–3 accessible books shaping public understanding of intelligence.
16. **Popular Science Book:** Write a best-selling, serious popular science book on machine intelligence, compositionality, and AI-driven scientific discovery.

### Pillar V: Mentorship, Grants, Leadership & Legacy
8. **Elected Fellowships:** Become an elected Fellow of ACM, IEEE, SIAM, APS, and receive 1–2 honorary doctorates.
24. **Scholarly Mentorship:** Deeply mentor 10–20 PhD students and 50+ undergraduates over career; mentor thousands indirectly via open materials.
25. **Endowed Student Fellowship:** Establish a fellowship/prize funding 5–10 underrepresented students annually in math, ML, and scientific AI.
26. **Policy & Advisory Leadership:** Serve on national AI advisory councils and international scientific committees by age 40.
27. **Turing Award / Nobel Prize Recognition:** Target the Turing Award (learning theory/algorithms) or Nobel Prize (AI-driven discovery in physics/chemistry/medicine). Early-career targets: Sloan Fellowship, NSF CAREER, ACM Doctoral Dissertation Award.
28. **Research Funding ($5M–$50M):** Secure $5–20M in cumulative PI research grants to sustain a world-class lab (scaling to $50M for an AI-for-Science center).
29. **Permanent Archive & Biography:** Maintain a verified multilingual Wikipedia presence, oral history archive, and permanent open code/paper repositories.
30. **Endowed Institute / Legacy Archive:** Curate a digital library of 2,000–5,000 foundational works, donate physical/digital archives to a university, and establish an enduring research institute.
