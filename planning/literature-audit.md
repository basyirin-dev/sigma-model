## 2. PICO Framework

- **Population (P):** Deep neural networks (Transformers, RNNs, MLPs) trained via standard gradient descent.
- **Intervention (I):** Compositional pressure and dynamical regularization techniques.
- **Comparator (C):** Standard unconstrained training architectures and baselines.
- **Outcome (O):** Out-of-distribution compositional generalization, representation geometry separation, and loss landscape spectra.
