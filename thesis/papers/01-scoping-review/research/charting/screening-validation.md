# Eligibility-stage dual-screening validation — Paper 01 Phase 11

- Sample: 430 of 2867 screened records (15%, seeded random)
- Re-screen: operational eligibility rule applied to title+abstract (calibrated second pass by the author)
- Original decisions mapped as Include/Uncertain -> carried, Exclude -> excluded
- Raw agreement: 296/430 (68.8%)
- Cohen's kappa: 0.389

| Stage | Agreement | n |
|---|---|---|
| title-excluded | 128/198 (65%) | 198 |
| abstract | 168/232 (72%) | 232 |

Interpretation: this is a calibrated single-coder consistency check applying the same operational rule to a sample, reported honestly as such; it does not substitute for independent dual-reviewer screening, which remains a limitation.
