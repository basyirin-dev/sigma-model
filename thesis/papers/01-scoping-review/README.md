## Research Question (PCC Framework)

> **How is the landscape of AGI safety research structured in terms of subdomains, formal methods, and the treatment of internal representation structure, and what gaps exist that a schema-coherence framework could address?**

| Element | Definition |
|---------|-----------|
| **Population** | AGI/ML safety research community |
| **Concept** | AGI safety frameworks, formal methods, failure modes, and treatment of internal representation structure |
| **Context** | Peer-reviewed and grey literature 2015–March 2026 |

### Sub-Questions
- **SQ1**: What subdomains constitute AGI safety, and how are they bounded?
- **SQ2**: What formal methods and evidence types are used across subdomains?
- **SQ3**: How does internal representation structure feature in safety discussions?
- **SQ4**: What is the documented relationship between CG and alignment?
- **SQ5**: What gaps could a schema-coherence framework address?
