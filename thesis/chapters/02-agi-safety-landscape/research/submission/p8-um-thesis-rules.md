# P8 — Universiti Malaya Thesis-Submission Rules

**Institution:** Universiti Malaya (UM). Note: `research/manual-steps-needed.md` says "University of Melbourne" — leftover copy-paste error; PASUM is UM's Pusat Asasi Sains.

1. **Thesis-by-publication — YES, PhD-by-research only.**
   - "A research report, dissertation or thesis can be written in one of the following formats: Conventional format; Article style format; **Format of published papers (this option is only available for Doctoral programme by research candidates)**." (FSKTM Guidelines §1.3.)
   - Not available for Master's/undergraduate/foundation theses.

2. **Minimum papers / under-review status.**
   - PhD by Retrospective/Prior Publication route: **≥ 5** publications, published retrospectively within ≤ 10 years of application.
   - In-candidature route: papers must be "published or **accepted** by high impact journals (e.g. journals indexed by Web of Science)".
   - **Under-review (submitted, not accepted) and preprint-only papers do NOT count** at submission. Faculty variants: **UNVERIFIED**.

3. **Monograph format.**
   - Conventional structure: ~6 chapters (Intro, Literature Review, Methodology, Results, Discussion, Conclusion); abstract ≤ 500 words in BM + EN; double-spaced; A4; APA references.
   - Published papers are **not** embedded as chapters — only in the "LIST OF PUBLICATIONS AND PAPERS PRESENTED" supplementary section. No page norm stated.

4. **Non-degree / pre-university (PASUM) student.**
   - UM thesis rules apply only to candidates **enrolled in a postgraduate programme within their candidature period** ("Submission … has to be done within the candidature period after title approval").
   - A PASUM student is not a registered postgraduate candidate; **no provision found** for thesis submission by a non-enrolled independent individual (UNVERIFIED; treat as not available).
   - Implication: the thesis-by-publication plan requires a future **PhD-by-research enrolment** (entry normally after a bachelor's degree; Malaysian fast-track post-bachelor's PhD possible — specific age/entry rules UNVERIFIED).

5. **Recommendation.**
   - Plan for the **PhD-by-Research thesis-by-publication format**, executed during future doctoral candidature at UM (or comparable institution).
   - Keep the 9-paper arc so **≥ 5 papers are published or accepted in WoS-indexed journals by thesis submission**, on one theme (the σ-trap arc qualifies).
   - Two structural warnings: (a) the publication-in-fulfilment policy requires **candidate first-authorship + supervisor co-authorship + UM affiliation** — a solo-authored corpus conflicts unless a written faculty exemption is granted (flag early); (b) under-review status does not count, so schedule acceptances within candidature.
   - Monograph format only as fallback.
