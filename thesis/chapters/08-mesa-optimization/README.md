## Mesa-Optimisation Diagnostic Criteria (required — assessment F7)

The chapter must operationally distinguish **proxy behaviour** from **mesa-optimisation**.
A phenomenon counts as mesa-optimisation only with evidence of:

1. Emergence of an **internally represented objective**;
2. **Optimisation** of that objective (not mere correlation);
3. **Persistence across distributional changes**;
4. **Distinction from the outer objective**;
5. Evidence of optimisation rather than correlation.

Without these, "mesa-optimization" is a label applied retrospectively. Low-σ must be shown to
**cause** (or mechanistically produce) the phenomenon, not merely accompany it. Includes the
goal-misgeneralization reconciliation with the σ-trap (assessment B5): is goal misgeneralization
a low-σ expression, and why did the Ch 2 keyword mapping miss it?
